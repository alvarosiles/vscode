## Summary

A simple commitment, no fine print:

- ✅ We don't collect personal data.
- ✅ We don't use third-party trackers or analytics.
- ✅ We don't send information to external servers.
- ✅ Everything runs locally when applicable.

## What information we collect

None of our extensions collect personally identifiable data. We don't require sign-up, accounts, or contact information to install or use any of our tools.

Official stores (Visual Studio Marketplace) may collect aggregated, anonymous installation metrics under their own policies, outside of our control.

## Local storage

The website stores your theme (light/dark) and language preference using the browser's `localStorage`. This information stays only on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Some extensions may save user settings (e.g. formatting preferences) directly in your local Visual Studio Code configuration. That data never leaves your machine because of anything we do.

## Third-party services

**Pro AI Assistant** is the only extension that can communicate with external services, and only if you explicitly configure it with an AI provider of your choice. In that case, your code or queries are sent directly from your editor to that provider, subject to the provider's own privacy policy. We don't operate any intermediary servers or store those requests.

The rest of the extensions (Themes, Tools, Snippets, Dev Pack) make no network connections at all.

## Analytics and trackers

Neither this website nor our extensions use tracking cookies, pixels, third-party analytics, or advertising tools.

## Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy, outside of our control.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly. We recommend reviewing it periodically.
